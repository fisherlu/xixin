# App Icon Design Spec

## Concept
"Breath of Calm" - A minimalist meditation figure in lotus pose,
floating within a deep forest-green gradient. Small breath/leaf
motifs suggest the flow of air and tranquility.

## Colors
- Background: Deep forest green gradient (#2D6A4F -> #1B4332)
- Figure: White, semi-transparent line art
- Accents: Soft white glow, subtle breath wave lines

## Variations needed
- 1024x1024 (App Store)
- 512x512 (Google Play)
- 216x216 (Huawei AppGallery)
- 48x48, 72x72, 96x96, 144x144, 192x192 (Android densities)

## AI Image Generation Prompt
"A minimalist app icon for a meditation app. Silhouette of a person
meditating in lotus pose, drawn in thin white lines. Deep forest green
gradient background with rounded corners. Subtle floating leaf shapes
and breath wave lines. Clean, calm, modern. No text. Flat design with
subtle depth. iOS app icon style. Professional quality."
